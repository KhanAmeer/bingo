B I N G O
2–6 Players · Custom boards · Turn-based calling

Choose your room size (2 to 6 players). Everyone arranges their own 5×5 grid before the match begins.

Rules
1. Fill your 5×5 board with numbers 1–25 in any order you like.
2. Players take turns choosing a number to announce to the room.
3. Tap announced numbers on your card to mark them. Complete your target lines and hit BINGO! to win.
